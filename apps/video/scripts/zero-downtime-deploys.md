# Script: "Zero-downtime deploys" (60 seconds, 8 scenes)

Target pace: ~150 words per minute, conversational. Bracketed marks are the
estimated time of each line — match this pace when recording so the animations
stay in sync. After you send the recording, timings get re-synced frame-exact.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:05)
> "One deploy. Twenty minutes of downtime. Caused by one missing line."

## Scene 2 — The old way (0:05–0:12)
> "The old way: stop the server, swap the code, restart. Simple. And broken."

## Scene 3 — The cost (0:12–0:19)
> "For ninety seconds, nobody can reach you. Every click lands on an error page. Orders, signups, trust — gone."

## Scene 4 — In-flight requests (0:19–0:26)
> "Worse: requests caught mid-flight die instantly. Payments half-processed. Data half-written. Restarting is gambling."

## Scene 5 — The insight (0:26–0:33)
> "The insight is embarrassingly simple: never leave the chair empty. Start the new version before you stop the old one."

## Scene 6 — Health-gated switch (0:33–0:42)
> "Version two boots up beside version one. A health check knocks on its door. Healthy? Move traffic over, retire the old. Slowly, safely."

## Scene 7 — Free rollback (0:42–0:49)
> "And if the check fails? Traffic never moves. Users never notice. Rollback means doing absolutely nothing."

## Scene 8 — Payoff (0:49–1:00)
> "That's it. Boring releases. Deploy on Friday afternoons. Follow for one DevOps lesson every week."

## Recording tips
- Small quiet room, any USB mic or earbuds (never laptop mic).
- Mic ~15–20cm away, slightly below chin. Peaks around −6dB.
- Read word-for-word, no ad-lib. Match the bracketed pace marks.
- Don't stop on mistakes: re-read the sentence, keep going, mistakes get cut.
- Smile while reading. Send the raw audio — cleanup happens in CapCut.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how my portfolio integration ("the Application") handles data when you use "Sign In with LinkedIn" or other LinkedIn API services.

1. Data We Collect

When you authenticate via LinkedIn, we may access the following information:

  • Basic Profile Information (Name, Profile Picture, Headline)
  • Email Address (via r_emailaddress scope)
  • Public Profile URL

2. Purpose of Processing

We use this data strictly for identity verification and to demonstrate DevOps API integration workflows within this portfolio. We do not use this data for marketing or third-party tracking.

3. Data Retention & Caching

In compliance with LinkedIn Developer terms, we adhere to strict storage limits:
  • Member Profile Data: Cached for no longer than 24 hours.
  • Member Social Activity: Cached for no longer than 48 hours.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. All data is processed locally for session management.

5. User Rights

You may revoke access to this application at any time via your LinkedIn account settings. Upon request, all locally cached data associated with your account will be permanently deleted.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact me via my GitHub profile or the contact form on this website.
